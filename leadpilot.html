Project Overview

Lead Generation

Management and tracking of leads potential inquiries that are automatically collected through business searches by category and region.

The challenge

Finding warm leads through Google

The challenge was to let the user locate leads through Google Business, automatically based on interests, and allow the user to send a direct message to the business owner to offer their services.

The main UX challenge was to make lead generation feel simple and accessible despite the large amount of data involved. The interface was structured around a clear hierarchy: search and discovery first, followed by an overview of results and finally detailed lead information for deeper evaluation.

The lead search experience was designed to help users discover potential customers through a combination of structured filters and geographic exploration. Users can define a target location, select a business category, and refine their search before exploring potential leads directly on the map.

The goal was to display all leads that exist in the system with an option to filter by lead statuses in order to give the user the ability to manage their leads.

System notifications screen, here I focused on the empty state and the ability to add reminders and notes when there are notifications.
