The Process

Redesign campaign management system

I was part of the design team that was responsible for implementing the design system in taboola campaign management system.

The process was in collaboration with the ux team and included several versions.

The challenge

Simplify the process in the most correct and intuitive way.

Design a user interface on the Campaign Manager screen and use a design system.

The goal was to think about the process that a campaign manager does and simplify the process for him in the most correct and intuitive way.

With the ability to see a breakdown by months and years of the campaign and understand when it worked well and when it worked less.

The design direction was to follow a clean and clear line with graphical data and let the user receive all the information relevant to a specific campaign in the correct way.
